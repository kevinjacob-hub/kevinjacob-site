about-me:$|

Product and operations leader based in Seattle, WA, with 5+ years across logistics, ecommerce fulfillment, vendor operations and returns. At Ferguson Home I turn fragmented, manual fulfillment and post-sales processes into automated, measurable workflows. I use customer-feedback and operational data to find the root cause, pick the right fix (training, process, communication or tooling), and drive it with Operations, Product and Engineering.

Before that I worked the floor as an acting supervisor at Ferguson Fire & Fabrication, and before that I founded and ran Galapagos Labs, an e-commerce marketplace on the Internet Computer. I studied Management Information Systems and Mathematics at UT Austin.

Outside work I design, 3D print and bake, and I’m an avid explorer: 20 national parks so far, plus trips to India and China. Take the road trip to see where. And every week I host Kevin’s Korner, a dinner or potluck for people from every corner of my life.

Faith is a big part of my life: I’m a Christian, and my testimony is below.
